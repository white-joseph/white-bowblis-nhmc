How to read it: HCRIS reports legal changes, so corroboration is a diagnostic,
not proof of a transfer of control. If category 3 (suppressed by baseline only)
is corroborated about as often as category 1, the baseline screen is hiding
events that look like substantive transfers. If category 2 (counted by baseline
only) is corroborated rarely, the baseline screen is counting events that look
like within-family restructuring.
